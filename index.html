More settings · for advanced users
You rarely need these: Difficulty sets the bands and the gain for you. Drag a knob up or down, or turn it with the mouse wheel or the arrow keys.
Click a card to hear its band boosted. Most bands have a vowel or a consonant to remember them by: a trick from studio practice.
Why vowels work, and how to practise
Your brain remembers a sound better than a number. So link each band to a sound of speech: 250 Hz is “oo” as in boot, 500 Hz “oh” as in go, 1 kHz “ah” as in father, 2 kHz “eh” as in bed, 4 kHz “ee” as in see. Higher up, consonants take over: 8 kHz hisses like “ss”, 16 kHz like “ts”. Start on Easy, then step up. Ten minutes a day beats one long session.
